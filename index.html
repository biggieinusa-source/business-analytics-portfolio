Loan Default Risk Prediction

Developed a machine-learning model to predict whether a loan applicant may experience repayment difficulties. The project used the Home Credit dataset containing 307,511 loan applications and 122 variables.

Tools: Python, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, TensorFlow/Keras, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Google Colab, and Gradio.

Methods: Data cleaning, missing-value imputation, categorical encoding, feature scaling, financial-ratio engineering, exploratory data analysis, and MLP classification.

Business value: The model estimates default probability and assigns a risk category to support faster, more consistent, and data-driven lending decisions.

Evolution of U.S. Consumer Retail Behavior (2010–2025)

Created an interactive Power BI report to analyze the long-term shift from physical retail to e-commerce in the United States. The project evaluates retail-sales trends, differences in online adoption across business categories, and the structural effect of the 2020 pandemic on consumer purchasing behavior.

Tools: Power BI, Power Query, DAX, data visualization, trend analysis, and business storytelling.

Key Findings

  • E-commerce sales grew faster than physical retail sales during the analysis period.
  • Online sales represented an increasing share of total U.S. retail activity.
  • Retail categories showed different levels of online adoption and digital-sales growth.
  • Online retail penetration increased significantly after 2020 and remained above its pre-pandemic trend.

Business value: The dashboard helps retail strategists, investors, policymakers, commercial real-estate analysts, and business leaders understand changes in consumer behavior and evaluate exposure to digital disruption.

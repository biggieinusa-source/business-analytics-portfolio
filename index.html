Data Analyst

I am a graduate in Business Analytics at Saint Louis University with a strong academic foundation in finance and business management, supported by more than five years of professional experience in financial services, lending, customer service, and data-driven decision-making. My background has strengthened my ability to examine complex business problems, analyze financial and operational data, identify meaningful patterns, and communicate practical recommendations.

I have developed skills in data analytics, financial analysis, risk analysis, and predictive modeling, with hands-on experience using Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI. Through my coursework and portfolio projects, I have applied regression, classification, data visualization, and predictive modeling techniques to loan-default risk, retail-sales forecasting, and employee-turnover analysis.

I am interested in applying analytics across industries, including finance, healthcare, technology, retail, and the public sector. I am particularly interested in opportunities where data can improve performance, manage risk, optimize processes, and support better business decisions. My goal is to combine my business and financial experience with analytical tools to transform complex data into clear, actionable, and valuable insights.

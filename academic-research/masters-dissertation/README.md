# Master's Dissertation: Cryptocurrency Adoption in Zimbabwe

## Overview

This repository contains my Master of Commerce in Finance dissertation, completed at Great Zimbabwe University in 2020.

**Title:** *The Adoption of Cryptocurrencies and Its Effect on the Modern Economy: A Case of the Zimbabwean Economy*  
**Author:** Gerald Hlabiso

**Institution:** Great Zimbabwe University

[Read the complete dissertation](Gerald_Hlabiso_MCom_Finance_Dissertation_2020.pdf)

## Research Purpose

The study examined the potential economic effects of cryptocurrency adoption in Zimbabwe. It focused on the possible benefits of adoption, the regulatory framework required for cryptocurrency operations, implications for liquidity and international remittances, and the broader opportunities and risks for the Zimbabwean economy.

## Research Objectives

The dissertation sought to:

1. Explore the potential benefits of cryptocurrencies in the economy.
2. Investigate the regulatory framework required for cryptocurrency operations in Zimbabwe.
3. Assess whether cryptocurrency could help address liquidity constraints and the cost of international remittances.
4. Analyze the opportunities and threats associated with cryptocurrency adoption.

## Methodology

The study used a positivist philosophy and a quantitative, deductive, cross-sectional research design. Primary data were collected using questionnaires and structured interviews, with participants drawn from cryptocurrency firms, the Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe and the Ministry of Finance. The analysis used descriptive statistics, charts and multiple regression in SPSS Version 26.

## Principal Findings

The study reported that cryptocurrency adoption was associated with:

- Potential economic benefits, including faster transactions, financial inclusion and currency divisibility.
- Opportunities to reduce international-remittance costs.
- Potential relief from some liquidity constraints when cryptocurrency is accepted as a medium of exchange.
- Possible government revenue through an appropriate taxation framework.
- A need for clear regulation covering cryptocurrency classification, exchanges, initial coin offerings, remittances, taxation and financial-crime controls.

These findings reflect the data, institutional environment and regulatory context examined at the time of the study.

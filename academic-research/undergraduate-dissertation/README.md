# Undergraduate Dissertation: Outsourcing and Corporate Performance

This repository contains my Bachelor of Commerce (Honours) in Business Management dissertation, completed at Midlands State University in 2019.

**Title:** *Impact of Outsourcing Processes on Corporate Performance: The Case of the Construction Industry in Harare, Zimbabwe*  
**Author:** Gerald Hlabiso

[Read the complete dissertation](Gerald_Hlabiso_BCom_Business_Management_Dissertation_2019.pdf)

## Research Purpose

The study examined the relationship between outsourcing processes and corporate performance in the construction industry in Harare, Zimbabwe. It evaluated strategic, transformational and tactical outsourcing as potential approaches for improving organizational performance in a challenging economic environment.

## Research Objectives

The dissertation sought to:

1. Investigate the relationship between strategic outsourcing and corporate performance.
2. Determine the relationship between transformational outsourcing and corporate performance.
3. Evaluate the relationship between tactical outsourcing and corporate performance.

## Methodology

The study used a positivist philosophy and a quantitative, deductive research approach. Primary data were gathered using questionnaires and interviews from respondents associated with construction companies in Harare. Stratified and simple random sampling techniques were applied. Data were analyzed in SPSS Version 26 using descriptive statistics and correlation analysis to examine relationships between outsourcing strategies and corporate performance.

## Principal Findings

The study reported positive relationships between corporate performance and each of the outsourcing approaches examined:

- Strategic outsourcing was positively associated with corporate performance.
- Transformational outsourcing showed a positive relationship with corporate performance.
- Tactical outsourcing showed the strongest positive relationship among the three approaches examined.
- The study recommended aligning outsourcing decisions with an organization's short-term and long-term objectives, capabilities and operating environment.

These findings reflect the sample, industry conditions and Zimbabwean economic environment examined at the time of the study.
